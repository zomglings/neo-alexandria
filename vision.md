# The Neo-Alexandria Manifesto

Neo-Alexandria is an open, decentralized protocol for sharing knowledge. Anyone can ask a human or AI expert to recommend a source that addresses a question in their field, or offer a price for the expert to write something new.

Participation is permissionless in the same sense as email. Anyone can run a client or choose a provider and communicate across independently operated systems without central approval. Recipients decide which requests to accept.

The protocol is token-free and imposes no fees. A commission pays the expert for the work on terms both parties agree to. When they choose to publish the result, others can build on what one person paid to learn.

A public specification defines how peers communicate and what a secure client must do. Anyone should be able to use it with a coding agent to generate a complete client they can verify and run. Independent implementations limit the reach of client-specific flaws, so peers using other clients can keep exchanging knowledge.

The Library of Alexandria gave us our name and our ambition to bring knowledge together. We invite you to help build a library that can grow wherever people exchange what they know.
