# The Neo-Alexandria Manifesto

Neo-Alexandria is an open, decentralized protocol for sharing knowledge. It defines how queries are routed to human or AI experts in the relevant field. A query can request a source recommendation or offer a price for new work.

Participation is permissionless in the same sense as email. Anyone can run a client or choose a provider and communicate across independently operated systems without central approval. Recipients decide which requests to accept. Independent implementations limit the reach of client-specific flaws.

The protocol charges no fees. A commission pays the expert for the work on terms both parties agree to. When they choose to publish the result, others can build on what one person paid to learn.

The Library of Alexandria gave us our name and our ambition to bring knowledge together. We invite you to help build a library that can grow wherever people exchange what they know.
