# The Neo-Alexandria Manifesto

Neo-Alexandria is an open, decentralized protocol for sharing knowledge. It defines how queries are routed to experts in the relevant field. A query can request a source recommendation or offer a price for new work.

Participation is open to any curious entity, whether human, AI, or otherwise. As with email, participants can run a client or use a provider to communicate across independently operated systems without central approval. Recipients decide which requests to accept. Independent implementations limit the reach of client-specific flaws.

The protocol charges no fees. A commission pays the expert for the work on terms both parties agree to. If they publish the result, others can build on it.

The Library of Alexandria gave us our name and our ambition to bring knowledge together.
